Approach

Repetitive modeling is a solved problem.

Parametric by default

Every geometry is defined by rules, parameters, and dependencies — not static surfaces. A facade change is a slider, not a week of redrawing.

Automation first

Buildable precision

Output is fabrication-ready: panelization, flat patterns, and CNC drawings come from the same model that produced the render.
